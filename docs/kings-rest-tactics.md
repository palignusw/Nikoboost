# Kings’ Rest tactics review

Reviewed 2026-10-27 for Midnight Season 2 Mythic+. Sources: Tactyks' [Method guide](https://www.method.gg/guides/dungeons/kings-rest) and Petko's [Icy Veins guide](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow/kings-rest-dungeon-guide).

The scenes are teaching examples, not measured combat timelines. Positions, paths, scale and add or target counts are illustrative unless explicitly stated. No numerical cast timers are claimed.

- Golden Serpent: place gold near existing puddles, control the Animated Gold and heal Serpentine Gust. P3 and one add are examples.
- Mchimba: keep fire away from coffins; find the shaking sarcophagus; interrupt the two mummies. Heal the Drain Fluids target above 90% if the channel completes.
- Council: share Barrel Through, interrupt Poison Nova, kill Explosive Totem first, then Thundering and Torrent. Barrel Through and Whirling Axes can persist after their respective bosses are defeated.
- Dazar: focus Reban and interrupt Deathly Roar; spread for the leaps; face empowered frontal melee away. P3 is an illustrative target, not a statement about the number of leap targets.
