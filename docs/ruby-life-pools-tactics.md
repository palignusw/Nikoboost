# Ruby Life Pools tactics review

Reviewed 2026-10-27 for Midnight Season 2 Mythic+ using [Tactyks / Method](https://www.method.gg/guides/dungeons/ruby-life-pools), [Petko / Icy Veins](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow/ruby-life-pools-dungeon-guide) and the [encounter journal](https://www.wowhead.com/guide/midnight/ruby-life-pools-dungeon-overview-mythic-plus).

Scenes teach responses and are not measured combat timelines. Positions, sizes, movements and event sequence are illustrative. No numerical cast timers are displayed.

- Melidrussa: group to bait Hailbombs, move away, place Chillstorm separately and resist the pull; break the shield during Frost Overload and interrupt Frigid Shard. Whelp icons are representative rather than a count assertion. The separate Chillstorm positioning exercise remains available at `/tactics/ruby-life-pools/chillstorm-practice`.
- Kokia: Ritual marks a player and summons a Firestorm; place it accessibly but away from allies, focus it, interrupt Blaze Volley, and avoid Molten Boulder and its terrain explosion.
- Kyrakka and Erkhart: place Inferno Spit away, watch the wind moving puddles, avoid the dragon's frontal, stop casting for Interrupting Cloudburst, and dispel the tank's Stormslam debuff. The marked player and wind direction are examples.
